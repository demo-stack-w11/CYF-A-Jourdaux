My Love of Perfume

Perfume is one of my favourite ways to express personality and create memories. A fragrance can feel fresh, warm, mysterious, elegant, or comforting. I enjoy discovering how different notes work together, from bright citrus and delicate flowers to rich woods, amber, vanilla, and spices.

My Favourite Fragrance Notes

I especially like fragrances that balance a fresh opening with a warm base. Citrus notes can make a scent feel clean and energetic, while woods and resins add depth. Floral notes can make a perfume softer, brighter, or more romantic.

My favourite part of trying a fragrance is noticing how it changes over time. The first spray is called the opening, but after a few minutes the heart notes become clearer. Later, the base notes remain on the skin. A well-made perfume can feel different in warm weather, cold weather, or on different people.

Notes I Enjoy

  • Citrus notes such as bergamot, lemon, and mandarin
  • Floral notes such as rose, jasmine, and orange blossom
  • Warm notes such as vanilla, amber, and tonka bean
  • Woody notes such as sandalwood, cedar, and vetiver

Fragrance Inspiration

Perfume bottles can also be part of the appeal. Their shapes, colours, labels, and packaging often communicate the style of the fragrance inside. I like exploring different fragrance families and learning about their ingredients.
